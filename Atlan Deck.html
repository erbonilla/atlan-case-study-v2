03 Problem & context

The endurance-tech market has solved two problems well, and neither of them is The Optimizer's.

Deep without empathy

TrainingPeaks · "the data." Periodization telemetry, threshold metrics, TSS-based load management. Assumes the user has the bandwidth to interpret a CTL/ATL/TSB curve at 9 PM. Has the rigor; lacks the empathy.

∞
Decisions surfaced to the user, daily
Empathy without depth

Strava · "the crowd." Gamifies training into public performance — leaderboards, kudos, streaks. Uses social comparison as the adherence mechanism. Has the warmth; lacks the depth.

0
Citations under the prescribed-workout's "why"
The Optimizer needs both sides — rigor and adherence support. Neither product is built for them.

atlan.SESSION SWAPPER
11 / 22
→ The Session Swapper

Three properties. One gesture. Silent recalc.

  1. Notification-reachable. The Swapper meets the user where the disruption arrives: push or in-app banner. No deep-link surgery.
  2. One-gesture commit. Swipe to accept the alternative, or tap "Skip today." Neither path is a fail-state. Both update the plan.
  3. Silent recalc. The week's periodization rebalances without exposing the math. Depth on demand: tap the metric label.
Locked notification copy

"Life happens. Want to swap tomorrow's 6 AM threshold set for a 30-minute easy aerobic on Wednesday? The week still works."

Sage 60 / Hero 40 on this surface. Empathy first ("Life happens"), action second ("Want to swap"), reassurance third ("The week still works").

atlan.OUTCOMES · HYPOTHESIS 1 OF 2
17 / 22
→ What I can claim — with a plan
Hypothesis · Tile 02

A 90-day Atlan cohort would show ≥15% higher session-completion adherence than a TrainingPeaks-style control.

Measurement plan
  • Design: between-subjects, random assignment, pre-registered.
  • Participants: 30–50 per arm, Optimizer demographic.
  • Duration: 90+ days · primary uses weeks 7–12.
  • Primary: weekly session-completion rate.
  • Stats: α = 0.05 · power = 0.80.

Effect size grounded in Teixeira et al. (2012) — SDT meta-review of autonomy-supportive interventions in physical-activity contexts (10–25% adherence improvements vs. controlling conditions).

Falsification

If the 95% CI for the difference crosses zero, the hypothesis is rejected.

Hypothesis, not retention curve. The mobile app has not been launched to users.

18 / 22
→ What I can claim — with a plan
Hypothesis · Tile 03

Wet hands inside a waterproof pouch complete "mark set complete" ≥40% faster in Wet Mode.

Measurement plan
  • Design: within-subjects counterbalanced.
  • Participants: 12–18 · real pool deck.
  • Condition: wet hands · sun glare.
  • Primary: time-to-completion across 5 sets.
  • Secondary: error rate (false-positive completes; missed completes).

Predicted from the Wet Mode spec floors: 320×360 px touch zones (≈25× WCAG AA minimum), 16:1 contrast (exceeds AAA), and a one-direction swipe gesture grammar.

Falsification

If the within-subjects mean delta is < 40% or error rates equalize, rejected.

Hypothesis. Real pool, real water, real wet hands. Stage 02 in the roadmap.

atlan.ROADMAP
19 / 22
→ Validation roadmap

Five stages. Sequenced by empirical cost.

STAGE 01
Closed beta
5–8 recruited Optimizer-demographic participants. Two weeks. Smoke test for the Wet Mode and Why-button affordances.
STAGE 02
Wet Mode in-environment study
Tile 03. Real pool, wet hands, real sun. 12–18 participants. Time-to-action and error rate.
STAGE 03
90-day adherence cohort
Tile 02. Between-subjects, 30–50 per arm, pre-registered. The expensive one.
STAGE 04
Bilingual cohort sub-analysis
From Stage 03 data: parity of completion rate between ES-primary and EN-primary users.
STAGE 05
SME audit of citations
Every "Why" modal citation audited by a subject-matter expert. Stage 5, not stage 1, because the citations need to ship first.
atlan.REFLECTION
20 / 22
→ What I'd do differently

Four things, framed in the conditional voice.

  1. Recruit before locking the persona. 5–8 Optimizer-demographic participants, unstructured interviews — before the persona ships, not after.
  2. Wet Mode study during, not after, the design. Time-to-action data would have either reinforced the 320×360 px floor or moved it. Either way, the design ships with proof, not prediction.
  3. Spanish-native copywriter on Welcome & Calibration before shipping. The parity-locked lines are mine; a native voice would have pressure-tested the rhythm and the regional register.
  4. Build the summary form of the case study, not just the full form. A two-page summary that a portfolio reviewer can read in five minutes. Belongs next to this deck.
