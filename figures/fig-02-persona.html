What they value

  • Scientific rigor — real biomechanical "why," cited
  • Time efficiency — minimum effective dose
  • Adaptability to real life over rigid scheduling
  • Sustainable consistency over intensity peaks
  • Bilingual respect — language is a choice, not a default
  • Calm aesthetic — focused, not aggressive

What breaks them

  • Soft shaming, fail-state copy, streak alerts
  • Cluttered, decision-heavy dashboards
  • Borrowed gamification: badges, confetti, leaderboards
  • Rigid scheduling that treats disruption as failure
  • Apps that don't work poolside — wet hands, sun glare
  • Motivational pushes, generic English-default UI

I don't need more motivation. I need less guilt when life intervenes.
