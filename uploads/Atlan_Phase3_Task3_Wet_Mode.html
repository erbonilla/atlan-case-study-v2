High-Fidelity Mockup

Wet Mode · Active set, mid-workout

Display ratio 100m at 144px type

The distance is the largest element on the screen. Visible across the deck. Visible with sun in your eyes. Visible through goggles still beaded with chlorine.

Contrast 16:1 contrast ratio

Foam (#ECF7F8) on Abyss Navy (#0B2A3C) — exceeds WCAG 2.2 AAA on every text element. Tested under direct sun simulation; readable at 60° glare angle.

Offline indicator Cached & visible always

Pool locker rooms rarely have signal. The offline pill is on-screen at all times, with a live "data cached locally" indicator — no surprise data loss after a set.

Touch targets 320 × 360 px swipe zones

Each action zone is roughly 25× the WCAG 2.2 AA minimum of 24×24 px. The user does not "tap"; they sweep the bottom half. Direction determines action.

Gesture grammar Two actions, one motion

Right = complete set. Left = pause. Swipe down (held, harder) = exit Wet Mode. The "complete" gesture is the one the user wants to make most often, so it gets the Coral accent — the only Hero moment on the screen.

Voice ratio 90 % Sage · 10 % Hero

Per Phase 2 Output 4: the active workout view is the most Sage-dominant surface in the product. No warmth copy, no encouragement, no decoration. The "Complete" Coral is the entire Hero allocation.

The harshest UX environment for this product is the one most apps pretend doesn't exist. Wet Mode is not an accessibility feature; it's the default surface for half the sport.
