What this 2×2 deliberately does not measure

Five omissions, named on purpose.

▸
Brand and design polish
A high-quality interface can partly compensate for a weak adherence model. The 2×2 holds polish constant, which is a simplification.
▸
Coach-marketplace dynamics
TrainingPeaks' real moat is the network of coaches selling plans on the platform. Atlan does not compete there in v1.
▸
Hardware integration
Wearables (Garmin, Apple Watch, Whoop) feed into all four competitors. The 2×2 treats hardware as upstream infrastructure rather than as a competitive axis.
▸
Geographic and language coverage
Atlan's bilingual ES/EN posture is a differentiator the 2×2 doesn't render. It lives in Output 3 and Output 4.
▸
Pricing tiers
All four competitors have tier structures; price-tier comparisons live in a separate document at Phase 5 if needed.

These omissions are not weaknesses of the framing — they are scope decisions. The 2×2 is intentionally narrow so it can do one job well.
